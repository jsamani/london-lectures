Public talks and events, many of them free · 804 events · updated Fri 20 Nov 2026, 12:58

About

A combined list of public talks and events from London institutions, gathered from their websites every couple of weeks. Click an event's title for details and booking.

The table

  • Multi-day events (exhibitions, festivals, series) come first, ending soonest first; then one-day events in date order.
  • NEW marks events added since the previous update.
  • A blank Venue, Format or Price means the listing didn't say; the event's page usually does.

Filters

  • Search matches titles, venues, institutions and subjects.
  • Subjects are assigned by an AI from each event's title, so a few may be off.
  • Click ♡ to save an event, then tick 'Saved only' to see your saves. They're kept in this browser only, and iPhones may clear them after a week without a visit.

What's hidden

Events that don't seem to be public talks or exhibitions are hidden, such as repeating services and socials, open days, professional courses, internal events, events only for teachers or postgraduates, and others an AI judges not to be public events. This is automatic, so mistakes happen: tick 'Show hidden events' to see everything.
